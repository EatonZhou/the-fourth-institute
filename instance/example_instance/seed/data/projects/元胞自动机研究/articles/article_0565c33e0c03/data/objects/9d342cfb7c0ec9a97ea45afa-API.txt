env.body is unchanged MaterialActionBody (15691ada); env.state and env.clock expose that real body.
env.begin_unit(action_code): scalar or 96x96 integer0..3. Bins none/gradient/euler_shear_x/material_shear_x. Registers CURRENT material once; newborn of this step receives no record. New registration closes preceding own record explicitly; first consume preceding completion. Pilot only one initial code3 unit.
env.observe_local(): original host public fields plus direct[4,7,H,W], message[4,7,H,W], message_support, completion_clock. Each grid location is local; no permission to reduce hidden/evaluator arrays into control features.
env.step(action[2,H,W], permit=ForwardPermit(...)) -> maps, communication_receipt, checks. Exactly one unchanged native body forward. Per-cell vector norm <=1. Same source geometry, donor motor price, basal/growth/death and finite256 input horizon. Communication settlement changes actual E/heat and therefore subsequent physiology. No old core/intent modified.
env.consume_feedback(decision_clock=env.clock+1) -> once-only {source_clock, unit_opaque, direct, message_unread, direct_support, message_support}; duplicate returns None, not zero outcome. source_clock is completed postclock and precedes next decision clock. Direct is cumulative live own evidence, NOT a fresh independent sample every read. Unread is newly received evidence since prior consume, less receiver deaths. Missing/dropped message does not become a negative label. Cumulative message is stored separately and must not be added again after unread is consumed.
FIELD AXIS: registered_mass, contact, motor_paid, basal_paid, requested_action_y_time, requested_action_x_time, exposure_mass_time. Contact/payment are mass-share descriptive histories from completed actual local steps. Not causal action advantages, net organism energy, or newborn return. Requested action-time is distinct from executed K/flux and actual payment. Four bins are finite action identities; opaque unit/source/clock IDs may deduplicate only.
TRANSPORT/TIMING: actual K first; add current completed local uptake/payment to registered old support; separate actor death then rule death. Both stage records use native retained actor/pre-rule arrays (exact stage algebra after the sole host call, not a second body advance or future inference). The two deletions partition one old registration; rule does not reuse already actor-deleted mass. No global death label enters a live controller.
DELIVERY: after actual host growth/death, each source/stage record is split across four periodic adjacent FINAL OLD survivors proportionally to their mass; no same-cell delivery. Priority actor then rule, +y,-y,+x,-x. Each nonempty accepted link costs 256 bytes *1e-7 E/byte/hop =2.56e-5 E. Full finite envelope per fractional share; no threshold sweep. No receiver or unpaid share is undelivered, never redistributed or retried. Incoming sums conserve every field (signed action-time included); envelopes/links are not independent samples.
PAYMENT: OLD-energy budget = max(K(old E)-actual actor energy spent,0)*actual keep, capped by actor_post_E*rule_keep. This charges spending to old energy first and excludes current uptake and newborn endowment. Deduct accepted charge from final physical E; add same amount to heat. Fee does not retroactively alter this step's already completed growth/death. Next step uses lower E. It never borrows future E.
OWNERSHIP: old direct and stored messages move via real K and old survival. Newborn gets no ancestor record; a later explicit new registration may include then-current material. Existing received messages lost with recipients are recorded only in evaluator ledger, never forwarded. message_support excludes current newborn. Stored records may exceed physical receiver mass because they describe other material; do not treat them as tissue or independent trials.
RECEIPT arrays: generated[actor/rule,code,field,H,W], incoming[code,field,H,W], undelivered_no_receiver, undelivered_unpaid, source-direction fractions/accepted[2,4,H,W], destination paid/requested/budget_before/after, actor_keep/rule_keep, receiver_old_survivor, direct_after/message_after/message_support, message_lost_actor/rule. Actual maps retain K/keep, old body/E, transported body, survivor/born/death/uptake, original detailed fees, plus communication_paid and energy_after_communication. Original maps.energy is PRE communication fee; env.state energy and added field are POST fee.
LOCAL LEARNER: own direct, legally received unread/content and its local support; original local public physical state. EVALUATOR ONLY: undelivered/dead source arrays, full generated ledger, all-organism E/U and registered death totals, receiver loss aggregates. Never fill learner targets from evaluator-only values. Terminal counts are known offline but unavailable locally when undelivered; explicit unknown refers to missing measurement, not known death or known nondelivery.
CHECKPOINT: save(directory) writes body, records, unread, support, unit/consume clocks and ledger to a pending directory then publishes complete directory. restore(directory) has no forward/payment. Restoring a consumed complete snapshot yields None on duplicate read, identical E/heat; restoring a pre-read branch is an intentional separate fork, not global exactly-once persistence. In-flight crash journal recovery is not implemented. Source ID = (opaque unit, completed clock, stage, source grid index); no world labels.
COST LIMITS: motor/basal/synthesis/template/communication heat are distinct components. birth_fuel includes endowment/chemical expenditure and is NOT another heat term to add to synthesis/template. Dead energy is separate. No extra compute/storage/learning-writing physical tariff is implemented; received-message fee alone does not establish fully priced learning. All controllers must share this wrapper and tariff, while actual charges may differ after their trajectories diverge.
SAVED: every actual main step has communication_POSTCLOCK.npz with exact code3-only nonzero slice and explicit action_codes=[3], stage maps, recipient payment, K/keep, body/E/uptake/survivor/born and material intent/cohort. Other fixed bins are exactly unregistered zero in this pilot, not missing inferred labels. Full physical maps and complete wrapper/intent at offsets1/128/256 when reached. No fabricated remainder for the fourth path. Last complete wrapper and intent are present even for its actual one-step end.
