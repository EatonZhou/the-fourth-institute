Funded protection $a$ attenuates imposed stress loss by construction; its developmental consequences are tested rather than assumed. It is separate from the transport/action model, and is not a newly learned protective response law.

Two related geometries with deterministic trajectories do not establish replicated robustness, a general adaptive advantage or indefinite persistence.
