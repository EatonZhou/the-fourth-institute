Temporal credit reassignment in continuing deformable bodies, with joint delta as a strong simple reference. (A) Native event schematic for the 40-step lesson: an early cue is present for eight steps, followed by a four-step gap, a recent cue for eight steps, another four-step gap, and four local outcome pulses at lesson steps 25, 29, 33 and 37 (one-based). The two cue events are independently crossed. In the first batch, cue meaning reverses after clock 640 while the early event remains predictive. At the continuation boundary, clock 1216, cue meaning is held fixed and the recent event instead determines feedback. Thus meaning reversal and a change in the predictive temporal source are distinct manipulations. The learner does not receive the hidden boundary, source identity or favourable-cue label. The first new-source feedback occurs at clock 1241, after 24 updates without a new informative outcome; the first new-source choice probe at 1377–1440 already follows 16 feedback pulses. This is not one-feedback recovery. (B) Native clustered-column chart of the number of original conditions passing every prescribed behavioural probe: all six acquisition/reversal/retention probes in the first batch and all three source-shift probes in the continuation. Every complete probe must have endpoint favourable-cue contact-mass share >= 0.65 and the body must survive. All methods begin with four related conditions, two fixed seeds times two opposite initial meaning orders. The separate prior-disconnection series retains one failed last-event trajectory and two failed fixed-exp16-trace trajectories; these are not revived or counted as successful continuations. The last-event and fixed-trace continuations pass 3/3 and 2/2 within their surviving subsets, respectively, rather than original-cohort 4/4. The fixed older-event delta reference passes 4/4 in the first batch but 0/4 over all three continuation probes, including failures followed by recovery in two histories. Joint delta and gated two-expert learning both pass 4/4 in both batches: these results do not establish an advantage of gating. (C) Native point-and-line chart of the gated learner's old-source weight in all four conditions at clocks 1216 and 1792. The old-source weights fall from 0.99925–0.99952 to 0.00996–0.04007. Symbols distinguish seed/order combinations; straight segments connect endpoints only and do not depict sampled intervening trajectories. Seeds are 202610197403 and 202610197404, abbreviated 7403 and 7404. These weights describe two-candidate temporal-source allocation, not general causal discovery. Behavioural endpoints are M_good/(M_0+M_1), where M_i=sum rho*1[torus_distance to cue centre i < 2.5], in the final probe frame. They are region-contact mass shares, not Gaussian cue-intensity-weighted values, binary trial accuracy or whole-stage accuracy. The 17 surviving parents are continued from exact saved states without resetting or births; each algorithm retains its own body history, so between-algorithm comparisons are not same-parent counterfactuals. The two seeds and two orders are related contexts, not independent worlds. Local association and auxiliary computation/storage remain unpriced; controlled cue exposure, designed sensors and chemotactic actuators limit ecological interpretation.
