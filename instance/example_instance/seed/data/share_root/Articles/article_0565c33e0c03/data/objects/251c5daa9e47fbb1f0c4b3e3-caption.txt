Figure 7. Acquired conditional maintenance content has limited physical utility against strong instantaneous controls.

The host is an externally supplied, density-regulated material cellular automaton with centrally coordinated purchasing, not the original Lenia kernel. A cue precedes mechanical contact; locally sensed incident strain, including contact absorbed by protection, supplies the teaching label. Existing reserve absorbs demand before residual injury removes tissue. Ordinary conditional delta supplies the acquired content in all panels.

(A) Three readouts of two actual delta-acquired parent states, followed for 96 new steps with prediction writes stopped and the learned relation retained. Retaining, pooling or swapping cue-conditioned content changes allocation at matched realized reserve synthesis. Retaining content reduces tissue loss relative to pooling by 16.6% and 5.1% in parents 0 and 1. This is a finite content effect, not a new predictor. (B) All six predeclared arms from each of two acquired parents: continued delta, frozen acquired content, two fixed cue-to-risk maps, instantaneous allocation and uniform allocation. Bars show injury accumulated in steps 225–320, after the unannounced relation change at the 224 boundary; the preceding 96 stable steps are retained in the source table. Delta reduces loss relative to freezing by 9.17%/10.24%, but incurs 7.51%/4.83% more loss than the instantaneous rule. The accompanying delta-minus-instantaneous readouts show opposed uptake and terminal mass/energy trade-offs, without consistent net physical advantage. Neither fixed map is selected retrospectively.

(C) A separate assay starts from two actual 192-step states and retains the learned relation. Writes are stopped throughout. Each parent is branched into block/impulse contact and acquired/instantaneous readout, yielding all eight 96-step paths. The external strain integral is matched between a four-step block and a one-step impulse; changing bodies need not experience equal integrated demand. Bars give paired percentage injury reductions relative to instantaneous allocation. Uptake and terminal mass and energy are modestly greater in all four pairs. Impulse increases the absolute avoided-loss contrast by 0.271/0.028 mass units relative to block, but the proportional benefit does not increase in both parents. This stable-relation expression assay is not evidence of reversal success or isolation of a single timing mediator.

Blue and orange identify the two source parents within each assay; their branches are correlated conditions, not independent replicates. Matched synthesized material and synthesis price do not equalize holding, birth, movement or other costs. Fixed and instantaneous controls carry unused predictor capacity and its fees. Registered old-cohort death, cumulative births and retained newborn tissue are distinct quantities; turnover does not establish newborn-specific necessity. Supply, sensing, central coordination and computation are not fully physically priced. The earlier risk arm's missing 128-step tail is not imputed. No result is claimed for an unexecuted successor mechanism.
