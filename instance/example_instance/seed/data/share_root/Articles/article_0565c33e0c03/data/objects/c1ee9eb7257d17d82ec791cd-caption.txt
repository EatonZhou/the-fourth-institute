Finite tissue replacement in a regenerative host with fixed physiology. A, Four saved density fields show the prescribed initial ring and its finite continuation under original-greedy and uniform deletion layouts, with a common crop and density scale. This is not ring formation from a compact founder. B, All sixteen trajectories are retained: two input tapes, four initial bodies and two layouts. Only the four ring trajectories pass all four 64-step maintenance windows; original greedy already supports this finite renewal capability. C, Surviving founder and later-born masses at step 256 show actual replacement. Born after 128 is a subset of Born after life zero, not an additional disjoint cohort. These are related contexts, not sixteen independent ecological replicates. The 256-step result establishes neither a steady state nor learned renewal. Cue learning in Figures 1–2 was tested in a different, non-renewing host; those bodies were not continued into this experiment.
