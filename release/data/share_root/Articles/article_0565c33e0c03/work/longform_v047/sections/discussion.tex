\subsection*{Acquired content has a function without a demonstrated new-method advantage}

The central positive result is that a moving conservative body can acquire a useful action direction from actual local scalar consequences without observing physical displacement or receiving a reward gradient. The same actual bodies support interventions that alter their later movement when the acquired direction is cleared or rotated. These results give a concrete meaning to acquired content: it changes a physical future under a stated interface. They do not require a new optimization algorithm, and they do not establish that all of the later favourable occupancy is caused by that content. One cleared parent still passes its aftereffect, whereas the reward-reversal interval succeeds in only one of the two original conditions.

This distinction matters for the interpretation of artificial-life learning. A flexible body, an online update and an appealing trajectory do not by themselves identify the capability that was learned. The scalar rule acquires a reward-related response direction within an engineered architecture of motion, cohesion, energy transduction and exploration. The richer gradient-conditioned model acquires response gain while receiving its direction. The motion-observing IV and affine models estimate a local execution response while receiving displacement and a target gradient. Each can be useful under its own information conditions. The restricted scalar result is the primary action evidence here because fewer observations already encode the required direction, not because its measured performance is uniformly greater.

The execution-change comparison further narrows the role of continued learning. Frozen estimates eventually lose useful occupancy, while continued response estimation preserves it. Yet a fixed run-and-tumble rule initialized with the same acquired direction also preserves it, at lower motion expenditure. The comparison demonstrates a need for a responsive process in these futures, not a unique need for continuous fitting or a benefit of a new estimator. Higher scores under continued estimation are real, but two related parents and different realized costs do not support a broad ranking. Standard strong controls are part of the explanation rather than obstacles to a desired learning claim.

\subsection*{Information access, content intervention and continued action are different tests}

Earlier gradient-conditioned experiments expose a particularly clear alternative to relearning. A new lawful observation can turn a command through an already acquired positive gain while the gain writer remains frozen. In a periodic environment, continued motion along the old direction can also end in favourable territory after a relabelling. The terminal occupancy criterion describes that outcome correctly, but identifying revision requires the actual trajectory, observation timing and a matched unchanged-relation future. Neither frozen-response turning nor periodic return should be relabelled as acquisition of a new mapping.

The scalar experiment removes those supplied directional observations, but physical registration remains consequential. Its perturbation, starting receipt and response state travel through the same material mixture as the body. This makes them locally available without a central reward pool. It also means a transported score is mixed across contributors, and a completed local outcome difference is not an unbiased whole-body causal gradient. The measurements establish a finite algorithmic capability under that implementation. A unique advantage of material attachment over equally informed alternatives is not established by the near-matched carried and lattice-stored IV comparison.

Historical-order experiments make an analogous point about representation. Exchanging early cue records while holding present body and learned action values fixed changes real motor commands. The past therefore matters beyond the current terminal cue. Nevertheless, a literal history table with sufficient capacity already solves the observed strings, while a fixed gap filter relearns them faster. The latter is a useful prior over task invariance, not evidence that the additional representation was itself discovered. The weak performance of one current-body encoding is likewise not a theorem about all embodied memory. The experiment identifies content dependence and a strong simple explanation within a centrally instrumented, non-renewing host.

The material-carried history experiment goes further than the central table by locating memory and online readout updates in the transported body. Its fixed local delay line learns an initially negative relationship and succeeds across unseen blank intervals and after retraining. Content interventions alter actual paid actions. Nevertheless, ordinary online readout over fixed memory already suffices, and the tested recurrent-gain plasticity does not improve that capability. Returning the gain to its initial value has no action effect in the four declared parents. This is a negative result for that candidate under its tested interface, not a general rejection of learning in bodily dynamics. The first failure to maintain stimulus contact and the subsequent paid physical return also show why sensory access must be separated from storage capacity.

The compliance experiment provides a stronger, exact explanation of another proposed physical increment. A learned coefficient changes real transport, yet a fixed-material local readout can algebraically reproduce the same force response and does so along all three measured histories. Sign interventions establish a role for that response content, while clearing and mirroring provide no consistent residual shape-memory advantage. Calling a coefficient a material property therefore does not establish extra capability under a control interface that can implement the same transformation. Changing force authority or pricing complete work would define a different comparison, for which this manuscript has no result.

The separate elastic-contact network reaches a related conclusion through actual rest-length changes. Ordinary local uptake feedback leaves content that modestly improves later resource contact, while a matched perturbation-only control preserves the same exploration. The benefit survives the environmental shift in only two of three histories. A force-equivalent readout reproduces the main plastic trajectories, and the main candidate never rewires its acquired network. These results restrict the useful effect to existing-contact parameters under an external support and load. Removing such content changes elastic energy as well as force; paid removal is a physical decomposition, not an isolated erasure of information. The completed contact-feedback extension separates that access question from utilization: distal consequences reach material with no local uptake, and the received signal preserves useful fixed-action ordering, yet the tested mean-age rule and slower pacing do not yield reliable revision. Removing storage saturation does not reverse the negative diffusion-versus-no-signal comparison. Thus physical reachability and informative fixed-action responses are insufficient to establish successful closed-loop learning. Temporal mixing and interacting actions remain candidate explanations, not demonstrated mechanisms or general impossibility results.

These conclusions are compatible with substantial computational roles for bodies. Physical reservoirs, developmental organization, adaptive transport and collective interactions provide many ways to transform information\cite{nakajima2015,developmental,tero,colizzi}. Locally adaptive and runtime-trained cellular systems demonstrate additional forms of online computation\cite{khajehabdollahi,pdnca}. What remains necessary is to specify which transformation is acquired, what is supplied and which comparison isolates a functional increment. In the temporal-credit assays, eligibility and recent-feedback rules address an established credit problem\cite{florian,izhikevich,fremaux}; a successful joint-delta control prevents the success of an expert gate from being mistaken for a uniquely new capability.

\subsection*{Encounter structure limits the use and revision of experience}

Dense scalar payments and externally arranged cue lessons give frequent opportunities to relate action or contact to consequences. The fixed-field encounter experiments address a harder coupling: the body's own actions determine whether corrective experience occurs. An acquired avoidance can prevent new contact after a relation changes; a previously acquired location can maintain residence even when stored values are removed. Absolute exposure and retained mass are therefore needed alongside conditional favourable-contact ratios. A ratio with a small contact denominator is not successful foraging or recovery.

The earlier controls remain informative in this hierarchy. Shared exploration can preserve connectivity without restoring useful contact. A temporarily disabled readout can permit matched updates and reveal a small later expression effect. Central pooling can restore paid recovery in selected parents, while frozen writing may be better during a later unreinforced interval. A fixed brake can preserve contact without showing learned stopping. In an independent life-zero comparison, phase-wise successes occur in different conditions and their complete acquire--reverse--retain intersection is zero. These failures do not erase earlier conditional successes; they locate the uncompleted link between acquiring content, obtaining renewed evidence and sustaining appropriate action.

This link also clarifies the scalar aftereffect. Previously acquired response state continues to travel and act without new outcome observations. Both bodies eventually occupy favourable territory, but only one did so within the prespecified reversal window. Allowing a longer interval would answer a different question and cannot change that original failure. The same distinction applies to the affine and IV comparisons, whose aftereffects succeed after their registered reversal intervals fail. A continuing physical history, rather than a sum of favourable windows, is the appropriate unit of the behavioural claim.

\subsection*{Predictive maintenance has a physical value that depends on its comparator}

The regenerative maintenance experiments extend content intervention to real production and loss. Under an actual common purchase quota, learned conditional readout directs protection so that both acquired parents suffer less injury than under pooled readout. Subsequent uptake and retained mass also improve slightly. This result goes beyond a lower prediction error: the internal content changes a paid protective material and a later bodily consequence. Its return is still limited. Equal synthesis does not equalize all future expense, one terminal-energy comparison is adverse, and standard delta learning already supplies the useful content.

The stronger physiology comparison is decisive for the broad claim. After the relation changes, continued delta improves on obsolete frozen content, but it does not improve injury over an immediate response to completed strain and does not dominate the body-function measures. Fixed controls with both possible mappings are retained rather than chosen retrospectively. Moreover, these simple controls pay for redundant predictor capacity in the readout-matched design; their cheapest implementations could be stronger economic alternatives. A finite advantage over pooled or frozen content is consequently insufficient to claim a general net value of prediction.

Within a stable learned relation, prepared protection has a small benefit over immediate response under both distributed and concentrated contact. The mechanism is physically plausible because useful prevention is locally saturated, reserves decay and tissue can carry or lose them. A fixed purchase budget cannot guarantee the same realized prevention at every time and location. Nevertheless, the time-profile intervention changes a coupled physical process. It excludes different predictor-update counts by freezing the writer, but does not isolate one mediator or establish successful unknown-relation reversal. Anticipatory regulation and environment-dependent prediction have biological precedents\cite{mitchell,tagkopoulos,lambert}; those precedents motivate the question without turning this standard predictor into a new method.

Viability and task value should therefore remain separate measurements. Successful movement can cost more energy, and preparation can consume resources before it prevents injury. The lower-effort intervention likewise saves motion expense while changing uptake and energy differently in compact and softer bodies. Sequential resource constraints can expose a downstream deficit when an upstream restriction is relieved. These physical effects explain why simultaneous improvement in every total is too strong as a universal definition of adaptation, while also preventing selective use of one favourable outcome to assert overall benefit.

\subsection*{Material renewal does not yet establish continuity of learned function}

The three core cellular host classes delimit what can presently be joined. Kernel-driven bodies establish finite developmental, resource and inherited-rule effects. Conservative bodies establish acquired behaviour and content-dependent action without birth. The externally supplied density-regulated host produces and loses substantial tissue while supporting conditional acquisition and maintenance. The last result is a genuine co-occurrence within one implementation, but neither high newborn fractions nor accumulated production establish that correctly inherited newborn content is required for the behaviour. In the tested stopped-writing newborn branches, zeroed and swapped payloads also preserve the finite outcome.

Registered old-cohort death, cumulative birth and retained later-born mass make the physical distinction explicit. They are not interchangeable measures of replacement and do not establish one-to-one renewal of founder tissue when total mass changes. The supplied bath, incident-strain sensor, movement machinery and central purchase coordination also remain part of the model. None is evidence that the original Lenia generation rule learned the same task. The earlier protection-carriage, ring replacement and body-writing experiments retain their separate results and counterexamples rather than supplying missing stages of a single organism's history.

A historical advantage can additionally reside outside the learner. Extracellular inventories and body energy are exchanged along with historical packages; inherited update rules and initial states operate on another timescale. Fixed simultaneous coverage can explain some resource benefits without identifying every history effect. These cautions accord with biological distinctions between external traces, learned responses and persistence\cite{reid,kramar2021,boisseau,reiber}. Outer search can discover useful behaviour\cite{hamon}, and learned and evolved components can interact in embodied systems\cite{gupta2021}, but evolution of a fixed tolerance is not evolution of the capacity to learn during life.

\subsection*{What the present evidence leaves open}

The experiments have limited independent histories. The strict scalar series has ten new life-zero trajectories under two paired seed--execution conditions and ten dependent continuations. The selected-action history comparison has three underlying seed histories, shared tapes and eighteen dependent probes. The local-memory series adds six comparative seed histories and one opportunity history, with twenty-one dependent probes. Predictive maintenance repeatedly branches two acquired parents. Sites, time windows and descendant branches are correlated observations, not additional independent environments. We report condition-wise results and preserved failures rather than cell-level significance or unsupported population generalization\cite{agarwal2021}.

The next substantive problem is not simply to add more persistent variables. It is to find a situation in which acquired state changes robust coordination or physical maintenance beyond what an equally informed simple response can already provide, and to retain that use as the same body changes. The present work supplies concrete positive capabilities, actual content interventions and strong alternatives that such a mechanism must explain. The completed local-memory gain candidate supplies a negative extra-value result, while successor hypotheses concerning heterogeneous-body coordination and renewal-sensitive maintenance have no completed result in this manuscript.

More broadly, regulatory persistence, collective adaptation and environmental prediction illustrate how varied biological uses of experience can be\cite{acar,yang,lambert,mitchell,tagkopoulos}, and physical organization can alter what is predictable about a system's future\cite{predictability}. Our results address a restricted part of that space. Locally paid scalar experience can yield useful action; temporal content can control an otherwise identical present choice; and acquired prediction can guide actual protection in a renewing host. Strong controls show that these functions coexist with simple adequate alternatives and incomplete reversal. Establishing their robust joint operation in one evolving cellular individual remains the research objective.
