
\subsection*{Contact-mediated feedback access and its controls}

The reachability extension retains fifteen moving material nodes, external support and periodic loading, without birth or a cellular-automaton/Lenia rule. Only initially proximal material has rest-length actuation; all arms share that ability. Completed uptake emits signal proportional to uptake divided by 0.018. Synchronous transport distributes a fraction 0.45 to actual neighbours, retains a fraction 0.98 per update and caps concentration at four. Newly emitted signal cannot propagate until the next update. Each live node pays $10^{-5}$ energy units per update for processing. Signal is not nutrition. Locally available concentration and mean age can enter the update; source-resolved arrays are host-only diagnostics. No resource position, future pulse or environment-change label enters learning. The signal keeps evolving while parameter writing is stopped.

The ordinary bounded perturbation update compares completed-window concentration with an exponential running average. Mean-age alignment uses a 128-update history of actually executed perturbations; average signal age is not an action-specific causal tag. The slow protocol changes action duration from 24 to 96 updates and therefore changes both exposure and chargeable actions. Complete paths use 240 acquisition updates, 48 stopped-writing updates, 240 after the resource shift and 48 stopped-writing updates. The three sparse histories use independent frozen Bernoulli(0.125) resource-pulse tapes, common to all arms within each history. Origin tracking and the pulse tape are unavailable to the learner. No-signal and instantaneous-global arms change information access and are capability controls rather than equal-information algorithm comparisons.

Three parents supply twelve open/cut, writer-on/off futures; information cuts retain springs and current local signals. Three additional founders supply twelve paid $+0.08/-0.08$ rest-length futures across the two resource positions after actual resource-free prefixes. Thus the six batches contain thirteen independent founder/exploration histories, thirty-one complete 576-step paths, three 96-step prefixes and twenty-four related 96-step branches. Only the sparse subset supplies three independent resource tapes; these are not thirteen independent environmental distributions. All six jobs completed, totalling 20,448 new updates and 18.116153 occupied worker seconds, separately from the earlier contact-network study. The processing and remodelling tariffs are model proxies, with incomplete storage/computation and external mechanical heat/work accounting. No seventh batch or successful successor is assumed.
