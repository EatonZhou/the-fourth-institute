\subsection*{Persistent contacts and a force-equivalent material control}

The auxiliary overdamped elastic network uses fifteen existing units. Live units can form a bond at actual distance at most 1.15; bonds break above 1.65. Each bond exerts opposite spring forces on its endpoints, and repulsion also arises from actual proximity. Initial left support and a common periodic rightward load are external fixtures. The resource band shifts without warning from $x=5.4$ to $4.7$. Every twelve updates a common local random perturbation changes a current contact; a bounded ordinary update from completed endpoint uptake relative to its running average changes the long-term rest length. A zero uptake is an actual absence of resource receipt, not an imputed label at an unvisited station. The learner receives no resource-band centre, stage or future target. The local sensing and communication hardware is not fully priced.

For spring stiffness $k_s$, reference length $\ell_0$ and effective learned length $\ell$, the reference spring and compensator apply $k_s(d-\ell_0)+k_s(\ell_0-\ell)=k_s(d-\ell)$ along the same contact. They share information, allocated state and perturbations. Three main histories compare plastic, force-compensated, fixed, sign-update and passive rules. Three subsequent histories compare plastic updating with identical persistent perturbations but no long-term update; no learning-rate or force-authority search follows the null result. Each path has 180 acquisition updates, 48 with writing stopped, 180 after the supply shift and a further 48 with writing stopped. Physical uptake and loading continue in hold windows.

The content probes use actual parents at steps 180 and 408 of the subsequent three histories. Rest-offset removal preserves the current exploration component, position and mass initially. The positive elastic-energy increment and change proxy are paid immediately from endpoint energy; released energy is recorded separately. Compensation retains the old effective-force content, and one sham checks the complete state. Each of the thirteen dependent futures lasts 48 updates. Chemical-energy accounting closes numerically, but movement pricing remains a proxy, recorded discrete external work does not close a complete mechanical heat/work balance, and the compensator's virtual parameter tariff is not a hardware model. No thermodynamic-efficiency comparison follows from equal software charges. All four batches, including a two-path opportunity run, total 11,112 actual updates and 3.403083 occupied worker seconds, with no failed job or replay. Seven independent initial states/perturbation streams share the external protocol; their twenty-three complete strategy paths and thirteen dependent probes are not independent environments (Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:contact-jobs}).
