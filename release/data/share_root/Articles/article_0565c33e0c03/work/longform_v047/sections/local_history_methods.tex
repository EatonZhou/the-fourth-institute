The local-history assay uses a conservative, non-Lenia transport body with no tissue birth. Stimuli occupy a fixed disk centred at $(16,16)$ with radius 3.4. A fixed sequence generator supplies these protocols, but the local learner receives only actually paid local contact, not the stage, seed, environment clock or hidden mapping. Its history is never cleared at a START boundary. The whole-body fixed readout and common motor actuator remain externally installed. A six-update movement assay produces an actual executed binary direction and locally completed energy gain or loss; these lawful local receipts provide the learning target. No pooled total reward is broadcast into the local learner.
